ABOUT EVARA

Care that starts
with a moment.

EVARA is a Sri Lankan herbal tea brand built around a simple idea: everyday self-care can begin with a mindful pause. Our Ayurvedic Period Wellness Blend brings together six familiar herbs and spices in tea-bag form, making it easy to prepare a warm cup at home or at work.

We aim to present botanical ingredients thoughtfully and communicate about them responsibly. EVARA tea is a beverage, not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
