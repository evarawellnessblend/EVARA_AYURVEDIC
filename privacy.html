YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy

This page explains how EVARA handles information you choose to share when browsing our website or contacting us about our herbal tea.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy describes the current website setup. The website does not currently accept online card payments or maintain a website account or order database. Orders and enquiries are handled through WhatsApp.

1. Information you may share

You can browse the website without submitting your name or contact details. If you contact EVARA or enquire about an order, you may choose to share information such as your name, WhatsApp/telephone number, the product and quantity you want, delivery address, and messages needed to respond to your enquiry.

Please do not send sensitive personal or health information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

2. How information is used

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to:

  • respond to questions about the product, ingredients, availability and price;
  • confirm requested quantities, delivery arrangements and payment options;
  • communicate about an enquiry or order; and
  • help prevent misuse and maintain reliable customer service.

EVARA does not use information submitted through this website to make automated decisions about you.

3. WhatsApp and other third-party services

When you select a WhatsApp button, you leave this website and interact with WhatsApp. WhatsApp handles information under its own terms and privacy policy. Messages you send there may include details you choose to provide. Please review WhatsApp's policies and settings before sharing information.

The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, and ingredient names link to Google Search. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address or browser details when your browser requests their resources or opens their links. Their own privacy policies apply to those interactions.

4. Website storage and payments

The shopping bag currently works in the page while it is open and is not connected to a website order database. This version does not process card payments or collect payment-card details. Payment arrangements, if any, are discussed separately with EVARA.

The website has not been designed to intentionally store your shopping bag in a persistent browser profile. Your browser, hosting provider or third-party services may still process technical data as part of normal web operation.

5. Sharing information

EVARA does not sell your personal information. If you place an order, information you choose to provide may need to be shared with a delivery or service provider to fulfil that request, or where disclosure is required by applicable law. Only share information necessary for the purpose.

6. Security and retention

EVARA takes reasonable care with information received through its customer communications. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Messages may remain in WhatsApp or other services according to your settings and those providers' retention practices. Please contact EVARA if you have a concern about information you have sent directly to us.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to provide optional information. You can also contact EVARA to ask about personal information you have shared with us, or to request correction or deletion where applicable law permits. We may need to retain some information when required for legitimate order, accounting or legal purposes.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. Please do not send a child's personal information through the website or WhatsApp unless you are authorised to do so.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact EVARA

For privacy questions or requests, contact EVARA through our WhatsApp Business contact. Please avoid including sensitive information in your first message.

This page is general information and is not legal advice. Review it against your actual business practices and applicable Sri Lankan data-protection requirements before relying on it as a compliance document.
