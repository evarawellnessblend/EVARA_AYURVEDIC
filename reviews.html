Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about EVARA, preparation and ordering.

What is EVARA Ayurvedic Period Wellness Blend?

EVARA Ayurvedic Period Wellness Blend is a herbal tea made with six botanical ingredients, designed for people who enjoy herbal tea as part of their everyday routine.

What ingredients are in EVARA tea?

Our blend contains Hibiscus Flower, Gotu Kola, Cumin Seeds, Ceylon Cinnamon, Fenugreek Seeds and Coriander Seeds. Please check the product packaging for complete ingredient information.

How do I prepare the tea?

Place one tea bag in a cup of hot water and steep for 3–5 minutes. Remove the tea bag and enjoy. Adjust the steeping time to suit your taste.

How much does one pack cost?

One pack costs Rs. 450 and contains 10 tea bags. Delivery charges, where applicable, are additional.

How can I place an order?

Use the WhatsApp order button on this website or contact EVARA through WhatsApp Business. Share the quantity you need and your delivery or collection preference. We will confirm the order details with you.

What payment methods are available?

EVARA intends to offer bank transfer and cash on delivery for eligible courier orders. The available payment option will be confirmed when your order is arranged. For personal collection, payment arrangements will be confirmed in advance.

Do you offer delivery or personal collection?

Courier delivery is subject to the destination and courier service arrangements. Personal collection may be arranged in advance. We will confirm the courier fee or collection details before finalizing your order.

How should I store EVARA tea?

Store the tea in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Seal the package tightly after opening and follow any storage instructions on the packaging.

Can EVARA tea cure menstrual problems or balance hormones?

EVARA is a herbal tea blend, not a proven medical treatment for menstrual disorders or hormonal conditions. We do not claim that it cures these conditions or guarantees hormonal balance. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have persistent symptoms or medical concerns.

How can I contact EVARA?

Contact us through the WhatsApp Business link on this website for product questions, order assistance and delivery enquiries.
